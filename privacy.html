Privacy

Last updated October 16, 2026.

Halcyon doesn't collect your data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see your photos.

Camera

Halcyon uses the camera only to take the pictures you shoot. Nothing is streamed or uploaded.

Where your photos live

Your photos and their darkroom settings are stored on your iPhone, inside Halcyon's own storage. They are included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on, which Apple encrypts and manages.

Photos library

If you allow it, Halcyon adds your developed prints to your Photos library. You can turn this off in Halcyon's settings. When you import a picture to develop, you choose it in Apple's photo picker, and Halcyon receives only that picture.

What leaves your phone

Only what you choose to share or save yourself.

Deleting your data

Delete a shot inside the app (in the darkroom, or by pressing and holding it in the roll), or delete the app to remove everything it stored. Prints already saved to your Photos library stay there until you delete them.

Children

Halcyon collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
