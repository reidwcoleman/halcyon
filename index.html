Halcyon

A vintage film camera for your iPhone.

The whole screen is the back of an old rangefinder: a chrome top plate, leatherette, a viewfinder in a metal bezel, a quartz date back and a knurled shutter. You load film instead of picking filters, and you can develop every shot again later in the darkroom.

Swap the camera

Nine bodies: Chrome, Black Paint, Cognac, Safari, Mint, Rouge, Midnight, Walnut and Instant.

Load film

Twelve film stocks, and the viewfinder shows each one live: Honey 400, Sunday 64, Meadow 200, Coast 100, Instant 600, Super 8, Expired '86, Cross 160, Neon 800T, Noir 400, Silver 100 and Antique 1920.

Develop in the darkroom

Change the film and how strong it is. Adjust exposure, contrast, colour and fade. Add grain, light leaks, dust, glow, a vignette and the orange date stamp, then mount the print in an instant frame, a 35mm strip or a slide. Your original photo is never changed.
