Changing the camera

Tap BODY (the rewind knob, top left) and swipe through the bodies.

Changing the film

Tap the film box beside the viewfinder, or swipe the viewfinder sideways.

Editing a photo

Tap the print by the shutter to open your roll, then tap any frame to open the darkroom. Press and hold the photo to compare it with the original.

Developing older photos

In the roll, tap Import from Photos to choose a picture from your library and develop it with any film.

Shots aren't saving to Photos

Check that Save shots to Photos is on in Halcyon's settings, and that Halcyon is allowed to add photos in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security, Photos.
